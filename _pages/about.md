I hold a Ph.D. from South China University of Technology (SCUT). My research interests include large language models (LLMs), AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and natural language processing (NLP).

I am particularly interested in how language models can integrate external knowledge, use tools, and reason through complex tasks. My interests also extend to agent planning and multi-step task execution, as well as retrieval methods that improve the factual accuracy and relevance of generated responses. More broadly, I aim to explore methods for making language-based AI systems more reliable and useful in real-world applications.

I welcome opportunities to exchange ideas and collaborate with researchers and practitioners working in these areas. Please feel free to reach out using the contact information in the sidebar.

## 💡 Research Interests

- **Post-Training for AI Agents:** Improving the reasoning, planning, and tool-use capabilities of language-model-based agents through post-training.

- **Long-Narrative Reasoning:** Understanding long narratives, tracking events and relationships, and reasoning across extended contexts.

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Integrating external knowledge into language models to improve factual accuracy and support knowledge-intensive tasks.

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Exploring methods for language understanding and generation, with an emphasis on reliable real-world applications.
